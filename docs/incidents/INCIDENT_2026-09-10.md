# Incident 2026-09-10 — intermittent music dropouts on a Voice PE

Status: **resolved 07:59 — root cause: a wedged state on the Voice PE
itself, cleared by a power cycle; the stereo receiver it sat on was
exonerated by test.** Watch item: recurrence.

## Symptom (operator)

Music on a Voice PE plays 10–15 s, drops out, then resumes as if the
stream kept going without it (live catch-up, not pause/resume). Seen on
YouTube Music and Pandora alike → not an upstream provider.

## Timeline

- 07:25–07:34 — MA log: player `20:F8:3B:0A:B1:17` (= Voice PE
  **0ab117**, 192.168.1.181, the basement-theater unit onboarded
  2026-09-04) — `WebSocket closed, close_code=1006` every 10–40 s;
  `Late binary: skipping 1 chunk(s); late_by_us=1.3–6.7 s,
  queue=324–379/8192`; twice `Cannot write to closing transport` →
  `Broken pipe`. 160 ERROR lines in 40 min, all this connection.
- 07:35 — box health checked: load 0.9, 51 GB available, GPU 0%,
  no kernel/NIC errors, disks idle. ClickHouse (Langfuse) at ~55% of
  one core — noted, unrelated. The kitchen PE (0ac919) shows nothing
  in the same window.

## Hypotheses

1. **Wi-Fi path to the basement PE** (signal/roaming/2.4 GHz) — fits
   1006 closes + seconds-late audio + one device only. Leading.
2. Sendspin buffer too small for that link — the "late_by" values
   exceed any sane jitter buffer; secondary.
3. Host resources — ruled out by 07:35 numbers.
4. Provider — ruled out by the operator (both providers).

## Evidence gathered (07:35–07:45)

- **One device.** Over the container's whole 5-day log, Sendspin
  websocket closes: basement PE 25, kitchen PE 1, satellite 1 1. All
  25 basement closes are from 07:24 today onward — the first minute of
  its first real playback session. Same firmware on both PEs
  (ESPHome 2026.6.0), same MA player provider.
- **Not the box.** `top`: 97.6% idle (the earlier `ps` 55% for
  ClickHouse was a lifetime average, not a load). GPU 0%, 51 GB free,
  no kernel/NIC errors, disks idle. Four ffmpeg processes = MA's normal
  per-stream transcodes.
- **Not total Wi-Fi loss.** HA's ESPHome API link to the basement PE
  logged no disconnects in 24 h while the audio websocket dropped 25
  times. ICMP to the basement PE during playback: 0% loss, 8.7 ms avg
  (the kitchen PE's 132 ms avg is Wi-Fi power save on an idle ESP32,
  not a fault). Small packets pass; the sustained audio stream does
  not — the signature of a marginal 2.4 GHz link, not a dead one.
- **MA's side of the symptom matches the operator's.** `Late binary:
  skipping chunk(s), late_by 1.3–6.7 s` = MA discarding audio the
  player would play too late; the player then resumes at the live
  position. `close_code=1006` = the client side dropped without a close
  frame. A "Writer failed" at 07:24:48 and two "Cannot write to closing
  transport → Broken pipe" are the same drop seen from the server.
- Provider ruled out by the operator (YT Music and Pandora both).
- The PE firmware exposes no Wi-Fi signal sensor to HA (none in the
  entity registry, enabled or disabled), so RSSI has to come from the
  UniFi console.

## Operator change (~07:43) and result

Operator power-cycled the basement PE **and** moved it about a foot,
off the top of the theater's stereo receiver (a large metal chassis it
had been sitting on). Two variables at once. Drops before: 12, 16, 7
per 5-minute bucket (07:25–07:44, last one 07:43:23). Drops after:
**0 through 07:48** with the player still playing — at the prior rate
~15 would have been expected. Fixed; attribution pending.

Why the receiver is the better suspect of the two: the PE's HA API link
never dropped and its pings were clean all morning, which argues
against a wedged Wi-Fi stack; only the sustained audio stream failed,
which is what a detuned/shielded antenna or a noisy neighbor radio does.
A receiver chassis under a PCB antenna distorts its pattern, and many
receivers carry their own 2.4 GHz radio (Bluetooth/AirPlay/Chromecast)
inches from the PE. Strong whole-house coverage doesn't rule out a
local null at the device.

**Attribution test (single variable):** set the PE back on the
receiver *without* power-cycling, play for five minutes, count drops
for its MAC. Drops return → the receiver; stays clean → the reboot.

## Attribution test result (07:53:44–07:58:56)

PE back on top of the receiver, **no** power cycle, playing throughout:
**0 drops, 0 late chunks** in 5 min 12 s (prior rate ≈ 3/min → ~15
expected). The receiver is not the cause. The power cycle was.

## Root cause

A wedged state in the basement Voice PE (ESPHome 2026.6.0) that broke
only its Sendspin audio path — the low-traffic ESPHome API link and
ICMP stayed healthy throughout — cleared by a reboot. Whether the wedge
was the Sendspin client, the Wi-Fi driver's association/rate state, or
something else on the device is not observable from this box. Hypothesis
1 (marginal Wi-Fi at that spot) is withdrawn: the same spot streams
clean after the reboot.

## What would have caught it sooner / watch

- The signature is unmistakable in MA's log: `close_code=1006` bursts +
  `Late binary` for one MAC. A memwatch-style canary on that pattern
  (N closes for one player in 5 min → HA notification) would name the
  device before the operator notices. Candidate for the roadmap if it
  recurs.
- If it recurs on this unit: note uptime-since-boot and firmware, and
  raise it upstream (ESPHome Voice PE, Sendspin client) with this log
  excerpt. The `update` entity showed no newer firmware on 2026-09-10.
- The device has no Wi-Fi signal entity; if a future firmware exposes
  one, enable it — it would have shortened hypothesis 1 to one look.

## Next (operator-side first, single variable each)

1. UniFi console → the basement PE client (192.168.1.181): RSSI, which
   AP it's on, TX rate, retries/drops. That is the direct evidence this
   box can't produce.
2. If RSSI is poor: move the PE nearer the AP for one test session, or
   add/relocate an AP; check 2.4 GHz channel congestion.
3. MA-side mitigation to try independently: the Sendspin player
   setting "preferred format" (`preferred_sendspin_format`) for that
   PE — a compressed codec, if the PE's firmware offers one, cuts the
   stream to a fraction of PCM's bitrate. (The other Sendspin knob,
   sync delay, is for multi-room alignment, not dropouts.)
4. Re-test with the same log grep: closes per MAC in the session window.
