## Findings

### 1. Generic "play some music" sentences are refused (blocker)

Drew's condition: "play some music" must behave exactly as before.

`taste.said_request` calls a sentence generic only when every word between
the verb and the first stop word is on a fixed list. Any other word makes
the request "names something", and with a bare `music` query that is a 422.
Ordinary politeness, time, place and address words are not on the list.

Method: 305 sentences of my own (none from household transcripts), beyond
the builder's 39. Each was posted to `/music` as `{"query": "music",
"said": <sentence>}` on a scratch copy of HEAD and on a scratch copy of
`ff8a7a5`, with the builder's own fakes, and the two answers compared
(status, body, play data, picks recorded).

| Result on HEAD | Count |
|---|---|
| same pick as before M23 | 250 |
| 422 "I didn't catch what to play." where `ff8a7a5` picked | 55 |

Two of the 250 ("play the radio", "play some radio") pick differently from
a request with no sentence, on both trees: the station reading of `said`
that predates M23. Not a change.

The 55 refused:

- Politeness and address (20): "Please play some music, thank you." ·
  "play some music, thank you" · "play some music, buddy" · "… bud" ·
  "… dude" · "… man" · "Play some music mister." · "Play some music, sir."
  · "Play some music, honey." · "Play some music, my friend." · "play some
  music y'all" · "play some music you guys" · "play some music Nabu" ·

- Time, manner, place (18): "play some music right now" · "… tonight" ·
  "… this morning" · "… quietly" · "… softly" · "… loud" · "play some
  music, not too loud" · "… everywhere" · "… downstairs" · "… upstairs" ·
  "… there" · "… too" · "… as well" · "… instead" · "… already" · "… real
  quick" · "play some music while I cook" · "… while we eat"
- Taste wording (6): "play some music that I like" · "play something I'd

- Whisper forms (4): "play some musics" · "play sum music" · "Play some

- Debatable (7): "play some music and set a timer for ten minutes" · "play

"thanks" is on the list; "thank" is not. "Kronk" is; "Nabu" is not.

**On dev, dry run, real MA reads** (dry run, startup warning and "nothing

**What the model sends.** Two text requests through dev's orchestrator
(`/api/chat`, rule `music`, home agent). Dev's shadow log recorded the
arguments:

| Sentence | `live_request` |
|---|---|
| play some music in the Kitchen | `{"query": "music", "kind": "genre", "player": "Kitchen"}` |

So the model drops "tonight" and sends a bare `music`, which is the case
the guard reads the sentence for. The second request stopped at 503 before
the guard (dev's default player is unavailable); the direct request above,
with the kitchen named, shows the refusal the same arguments get.

Reproduce: `tests/.venv/bin/python -c "from tool_service import taste;
print(taste.said_request('Play some music, thank you.'))"` prints
`(False, 'some music, thank you')`.

The mutant run shows why the tests did not catch it: removing "thanks" or
"favourites" from the list fails no test. The list is pinned only by the 39
sentences.

A word list cannot be closed over what people add to "play some music".
The guard needs the opposite test: refuse only on positive evidence that
something was named (the resolver finds a strong match for the clause, as
in capture 1), not on the absence of proof that the clause is filler.

### 2. Plays Kronk did not pick were dropped on the real data (blocker by the item's definition; small)

The builder's 15 dropped rows reproduce exactly (below). I checked each
against Music Assistant's own records, read from a scratch copy of
`library.db` (`media_progress` and the append-only `play_history`).

14 of the 15 are Kronk's beyond doubt: on the pick's own queue, the ten
Cracker tracks 243 s to 2802 s after pick 18 and not user-initiated, the
Cracker artist row 1 s before pick 19, three playlist rows 5 to 6 s before
picks 14 to 16.

The 15th is the station row under pick 13 (`library://radio/263`,
2026-11-08). `play_history` holds 21 plays of that station between 416 s
and 759 s after pick 13. The picks table holds no pick of it in that span
(picks 14, 15 and 16 are three other items). Those 21 plays reached MA
some other way than a Kronk pick: a request by name, or MA's own UI. The
row's last play is one of them, and the rule drops it because it is the
pick's own item inside the 12 h window.

Effect: that station's `plays` goes from 0.015 to 0.0; its rank among the
51 stations stays 13th. No artist score moves because of it.

This is the case the builder lists under "what it cannot tell" (a request
by name for the very item Kronk picked). It is not hypothetical: it is in
the data.

For ids 18 and 19 specifically: no row of any artist on another queue
falls in either window (0 and 0), so their missing queue drops nothing
elsewhere.

### 3. The refusal names a nonsense "closest match" (should-fix)

`_stripped_request` feeds the whole clause to the resolver, filler
included, and names any match at or above `hint_min_score` (0.5). On dev:
"The closest I found was To music, thank you by Treason." (0.74). The
sentence is true as a search result and useless as an answer. It goes away
for generic sentences when finding 1 is fixed; for a real stripped request
the lookup should drop the generic words first.

### 4. Named plays of the picked artist inside the window are dropped, tracks included (should-fix)

On the builder's fake MA (faithful to MA's source on the points that
matter: one row per item, sticky `user_initiated`, the three filters):

| Scenario | Rows dropped | Cracker history weight |
|---|---|---|
| household asks for the picked artist by name 2 h after the pick, same queue, 5 tracks | 6 | 0.0 |
| the same, 13 h after the pick | 0 | 5.85 |
| household asks for an album by the picked artist 2 h after, same queue (8 tracks) | 9 | 1.0 (the album row only) |

| the same album on another queue, pick's queue is NULL (every pick before M23) | 9 | 1.0 |

The builder names these limits. The size is larger than "one row": a named
artist or album play loses every track. After Kronk picks an artist, the
household asking for that artist is exactly the signal fix 3 should keep.

### 7. MA unreadable during the three reads (note; not a blocker)

- An unreachable MA, a timeout or an MA error raises `MAError`; the build

- Load: 30 pick reads took 6.3 ms each (max 13.6 ms). At the cap of 100
  picks that is 300 reads, about 2 s, sequential. The whole build made
  1,886 MA reads in 3.1 s.
