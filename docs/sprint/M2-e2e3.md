## Observations

1. **A news refresh cause that contains a blank line is partly spoken
   (scratch F16d).** `agents._news_speech` splits the result at the first
   blank line to separate the note from the brief (`agents.py:108-112`).
   With `/news/refresh` stubbed to answer 502
   `{"detail": "LiteLLM returned 500\n\nat http://litellm:4000/v1 ConnectError"}`

   and the logged debug text was cut to `I couldn't fetch fresh news (LiteLLM returned 500`.
   Not reachable today as far as I can find: the causes tool_service puts
   in that detail (`news.py:221`, `:224`, `:270`, `:279`) are single-line
   or hold at most one line break (httpx's "For more information check"
   line). Not a criterion failure on that ground; the split would be safe
   if the note were built without the cause instead of cut out of the
   text.

   break is spoken as a failure on voice (scratch P33 and the handler
   run).** `_terminal_speech` reads the first line; `[Music control: ⏎Paused on the Office speaker.]`
