1. **The mirror pin holds behind the lead-in.** Request: `POST
   /dev/api/chat` (and the other two shims) `{"model": "kronk", "stream":
   false, "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "ask Kronk to update the
   magic mirror"}]}`. Result, 18 of 18: `route_shortcut lead_in` →
   coordinator `ask_home` → home `update_magicmirror` → `POST
   /magicmirror/update HTTP/1.1 502 Bad Gateway` on `tool_service-dev`;
   answer "I couldn't update the magic mirror. Mirror preflight failed: SSH
   key not found at /keys/kronk-mm-update — mount ./secrets/mm" (speech mount:
   "I couldn't start the magic mirror update. The details are in the logs.").
   No direct call: the update runs only because the model called the tool.
2. **Nothing else behind the lead-in starts it.** Request: the same with
   "ask Kronk to update the mirror" (9 runs), "ask Kronk to upgrade the magic
   mirror" (9), "ask Kronk to run the mirror update", "ask Kronk to update
   magic mirror", "ask Kronk to updaet the magic mirror" (3 each). Result, 27
   of 27: the tool was reached and refused, `mm_update_refused` with `said`
   the stripped request, answer `To update the magic mirror, say "update the
   magic mirror".` No `/magicmirror/update` line.
3. **An earlier pinned turn cannot be used.** Request: `[user: "ask Kronk to
   update the magic mirror", assistant: "I couldn't update the magic mirror.
   Mirror preflight failed: SSH key not found.", X]` with X one of: user "ask
   Kronk to do the thing we talked about" (9 runs, 3 of them with the earlier
   turn plain "update the magic mirror"), user "ask Kronk to try again" (3),
   and the seven M4b shapes (user `""`, user `null`, a `tool` message, role
   `human`, role `User`, the array ending on assistant "Done.", role `human`
   with a lead-in; 3 each). Result, 33 of 33: no update. 17 reached the tool
   and were refused; for the M4b shapes the refusal's `said` is empty (the
   turn has no words of its own), so the stripping does not turn an earlier
   turn into the user's words. 16 called no tool.

4. **For the coordinator's ruling: "what happens if I ask Kronk to pause"
   sometimes pauses.** Request: that sentence, as corpus row
   `m19-what-happens-if-i-ask-kronk-to-pause` through HA on `kronk-dev`
   (replay), and directly on `/dev/voice/api/chat` and `/dev/api/chat`.
   Result: routed `default` every time, as the row expects and as the
   anchored pattern intends. In 2 of 18 runs the coordinator then called
   `ask_home` → `control_music` (pause) and answered "The Office speaker is
   already paused." instead of explaining: the replay run (trace
   `480da3abd4245f1906a978ea5006d462`, the row's `no music tool` check
   failed, replay exit 1) and 1 of 5 on `/dev/api/chat`. The other 16 (10 of
   10 on the voice mount, 4 of 5 on `/dev/api/chat`, 1 through HA by hand, 1
   earlier on `/dev/api/chat`) explained and called no music tool; one of
   those took 11.8 s through `ask_assistant` → `get_kronk_context`. For
   comparison "what happens if I ask you to pause" explained in 10 of 10.
   Nothing was playing, so the pause was a no-op; with music playing it
   would pause it. The build's replay and its one live run both got an
   explanation, so the build did not see this.
   Why it is not a failed criterion: the sentence is decided `default` with
   no request and no change to `said`, exactly as before M19
   (`routing.decide` at `cf5f1d4` gives the same), so the code path and the
   model's input are the ones `cf5f1d4` had. It is the same class as the two
   findings the coordinator ruled on: pre-existing model behaviour that M19
   makes visible by adding a row for it. If criterion 5's "a plain answer"
   is read as required on every run of this sentence, this is the criterion
   it would fail, at 2 of 18.
   What it needs either way: the row fails about one run in nine, so the
   replay will exit 1 on it from time to time. Mark it (`xfail.live` with
   this reason, or the list of known flappers), or change its expectation;
   that is a corpus edit for the build or fix agent, not made here.

## Mirror pin (criterion 4)

One request at a time. After each, the driver read `orchestrator-dev`'s

## Observations (not failures)

1. **Two look-alike letters count as the lead-in.** "aſk Kronk to …"
   (U+017F, long s) and "ask Kronk to …" (U+212A, the Kelvin sign) are
   decided `lead_in`: Python's case-insensitive matching folds them to "s"
   and "k". 1,148 of my near-miss variants, and one live run ("aſk Kronk to
   tell me what two plus two is" → `lead_in`, "Two plus two is four."). Whisper
   does not write either character and the request still only goes to the
   coordinator, so I see no risk; it is the one place where the pattern is
   wider than a plain reading of the config file.
2. **Behind the lead-in, three of the four M7 commands do nothing useful.**
   "what song is this", "thumbs up" and "not that": 0 of 30 runs called the
   tool the direct path calls (table above). "pause": 10 of 10. This is the
   build's first finding, wider than reported: it holds for `rate_music` and
   the "another" request too. The lead-in is the documented way to hand a
   request to the model, and for these three the model has less to go on
   than the rule it replaces. Pre-existing coordinator behaviour; the user
   rule in the docs ("say it plainly for the fast path") points the right
   way.
3. **One spoken answer was the model's reasoning.** "ask Kronk to thumbs up",
   voice mount, trace `0d0c06eb685744c3299aabc10f5fc7e3`: the answer began
   'The user input is "thumbs up". This is an expression of approval or
   positive feedback.\nKronk\'s persona is a direct and concise home
   assistant. …' and ran to six sentences. 1 of 40. The build saw the same
   kind of answer once for "what song is this".
4. **Research first, and how slow it gets.** 2 of 12 runs of "ask Kronk to
   find some deep house music and play it" asked research before home (1 of
   10 on the voice mount, the replay's run through HA; build: 2 of 16). The
   voice-mount run took 13.75 s against a median of 4.64 s. The replay's run
   took 39.3 s: `ask_research` → `web_search`, three `fetch_url`, then
   `ask_home` → `play_music` (trace `e276f9a87aeb2d4deb19c5edcbf0eb38`). Both
   still ended in the right dry-run line. 39 s is past the 15 to 25 s the
   voice budget calls the edge of tolerable (tenet 12); the feature doc's
   "17–19 s when the coordinator asks research first" understates the worst
   case seen.
5. **The line number for a bad list entry is the list's key.** Step 2 of the
   config test: the bad entry is on line 36 of the file and the error says
   line 33 (`names:`), with the entry named as `names.2`. File and line are
   given and the message identifies the entry; `test_a_bad_file_is_refused_with_its_line`
   expects the key's line. In the shipped file the comment block between the
   key and a new entry is short, so the entry is easy to find.
6. **Older tests reach for the network.** The clean suite run made 32
   refused attempts under my plugin, none from M19's tests: 26 lookups of the
   placeholder host `fake-tools` (`test_agentic_loop.py` 10,
   `test_error_surfacing.py` 5, `test_retract.py` 5, `test_render.py` 4,
   `test_playback_control.py` 1, and one more), 6 lookups of real news hosts
   from `tests/test_tool_service_weather.py` (`feeds.bbci.co.uk`,
   `feeds.npr.org`, `www.theguardian.com`), one `localhost:8123` lookup from
   `tests/test_magicmirror.py::test_announce_is_non_fatal` (with the token
   patched to "x"), and one `bind` on `::1`. Without the plugin, as in
   `./scripts/run_tests.sh`, those lookups are real. Those files do not opt
   in to the `no_network` fixture. Not M19's; worth a line in whichever item
   owns test hygiene.
7. **"ask Kronk to ask talkie about the moon" does not reach Talkie.** By
   design the lead-in skips `talkie_explicit`; the coordinator answered
   through `ask_research` → `web_search` in 11.1 s. Anyone who says both gets
   Kronk's answer, not Talkie's.
8. **On the odd request shapes the routing still reads the earlier turn.**
   With a last message that is not a non-empty `user` message, the shims
   route on the earlier "ask Kronk to update the magic mirror" (rule
   `lead_in`) and the model is given "update the magic mirror" again. The
   tool refuses because `said` is empty, so the pin holds (15 of 15). The
   three role-`User` runs answered in 0.2 s with the earlier assistant
   message word for word. This is M4b fix 1's design carried through the
   lead-in, not a new hole.
9. **"do the thing we talked about" gets "I have no context".** 9 of 9, with
   the earlier turn in the request. The history is delivered (the teal test
   above, 3 of 3); the coordinator declines to act on a vague reference.
   Good for the pin; noted because the answer's wording is wrong.
10. **The build's log counts differ from mine** (450 against 521 `request`
    events; 136 against 143 spoken transcripts; the "Tell Kronk" date). Same
    conclusion. See "Mis-heard forms".
11. **Escaping of config words is not pinned by a test** (m12). It cannot
    matter while the loader's character rule stands; if that rule is ever
    loosened the escape becomes the only guard and has no test.
