## Observations that are not failures

**O1. On Hey Jarvis, play, pause and skip requests that HA handles locally
are real, not dry.** `kronk-dev` has `prefer_local_intents: true`, like
`kronk`. HA's sentence-match debug (`conversation/agent/homeassistant/debug`,
read-only, executes nothing), as the office device:

- "play some jazz in the office" → `source: trigger`, template
  `(play|put on|throw on|listen to) some {media_name} [(in|on) [the ]{area_or_player_name}]`
  (the music blueprint automation);
- "play Kind of Blue by Miles Davis" → `source: trigger`;
- "pause the music" → builtin `HassMediaPause`; "next song" → builtin
  `HassMediaNext`; "set a timer for 5 minutes" → `HassStartTimer`;
- "what is playing in the office", "stop", "what is the capital of Peru" → no
  local match, so these reach Kronk dev.

So a spoken "Hey Jarvis, play …" is played by the blueprint through Music
Assistant before `orchestrator-dev` is asked, and `MUSIC_DRY_RUN` never sees
it. The plan's step 2 ("so experiments do not start music in the house unless
asked to") holds for `/dev/api/chat`, `/dev/voice/api/chat` and anything that
reaches `tool_service-dev`, and does not hold for blueprint-matched sentences
on the dev wake word. The same applies to an M10 corpus replay through the
`kronk-dev` pipeline. The build report says this too (its deviation 3). It
was not triggered in this session. Options: set `prefer_local_intents false`
on `kronk-dev` (websocket `assist_pipeline/pipeline/update`; then timers and
"what time is it" on Hey Jarvis also go to Kronk dev), or accept it and keep
music off Hey Jarvis. Decision for Drew or the coordinator, before M10's
replay script is designed.
